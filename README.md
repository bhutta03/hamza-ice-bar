# 🍦 Hamza Ice Bar

Official website for **Hamza Ice Bar**, an ice cream shop located in **Ali Sher Wala Bazar, Gujranwala, Pakistan**.

The website provides customers with an easy way to explore our range of **Wall’s and Hico ice creams**, discover popular products, find our location, and contact the shop for current stock and availability.

> **A little happiness, served cold. 🍨**

## 🌟 About Hamza Ice Bar

Hamza Ice Bar is your local destination for refreshing ice creams in Gujranwala.

We offer a variety of products from popular ice cream brands including:

* 🍦 Wall’s
* 🍨 Hico
* 🍫 Magnum
* 🌋 Wall’s Volcano
* 🍦 Cornetto
* 🍧 Ice cream cups
* 🍨 Ice cream tubs
* 🍭 Ice cream sticks
* And more

Product availability and prices may vary, so customers can contact the shop for the latest stock information.

## ✨ Website Features

* Modern and responsive user interface
* Wall’s and Hico product catalogue
* Brand-based product filtering
* Product images and categories
* Featured Wall’s Volcano promotion
* Mobile-friendly responsive design
* Direct phone contact
* Google Maps location link
* Dine-in and takeaway information
* Fast static website with no backend required

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

The website is built using:

* **HTML5** — website structure
* **CSS3** — styling and responsive design
* **JavaScript** — catalogue filtering and interactions
* **WebP / SVG** — optimized images and graphics

No framework or backend is required.
